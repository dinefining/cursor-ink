## Petri

Petri is a growing lab of living effects. Every square in the grid holds one. Your cursor is the lens, the petri dish: hover a square and the dish takes on its effect, click and it sticks. Stuck effects run together, top to bottom, on whatever is under the lens: the page itself, words you type, an image or a video.

A [Maybe Machine](https://maybemachines.com) by [Ravi Popat](https://ravipopat.info).
